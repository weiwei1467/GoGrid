GoGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

GoGrid is designed to work offline and collect as little information as possible.

Information We Collect

GoGrid does not collect personal information. The app does not use accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or cloud sync.

Progress Data

GoGrid saves learning progress on your device, including completed puzzles, learned words, score, and settings. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Purchases

GoGrid offers an optional lifetime unlock through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Audio

GoGrid may use Apple text-to-speech on your device to pronounce Vietnamese words and phrases when bundled recordings are not available.

Children

GoGrid is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact: sendtomenic@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same Privacy Policy URL.
