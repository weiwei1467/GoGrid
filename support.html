GoGrid Support

Help for GoGrid, the Vietnamese phrase puzzle app.

Contact

Email support: sendtomenic@gmail.com

Purchases

GoGrid uses Apple In-App Purchase for the optional lifetime unlock. If you bought premium on this Apple ID, open GoGrid, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases.

Progress

Progress is stored on your device. You can reset progress in Settings.

Audio

Pronunciation uses bundled recordings when available and Apple Vietnamese text-to-speech as a fallback.
